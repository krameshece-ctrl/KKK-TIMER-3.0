Using instructions

using the timer

mobile users

I am going to explain how to start and stop the timer for mobile users here. Scramble the cube exactly as per given above. hold the timer for 0.5sec. When you see green light, you can release and start solving your cube. After solving your cube press anywhere to stop the timer.

computer or laptop users

I am going to explain how to start ans stop the timer for computer or laptop users here. Scramble the cube exactly as per given above. Click your mouse in the timerto start the timer. You can release and solve the cube when you see grrn light. After solving the cube, click anywhere to stop the timer.

music controls

You have 50 music to choose. You can choose the music and solve peacefully

This is for cubers who don't like music or need to solveconcentrated without music. Please click the off button and continue solving

sessions instructions

You can name or change the name of the session. You can also go to the new session for differenciation. You can continue different cubes in different sesssions.

different cubes

There are different cube types in this website. Choose the cube type you are going to practice

inspection time

Please go to the settings. Please click enable features and click inpection time if you want inspection time. You can turn off if you really don't want.
