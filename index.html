Introduction

Welcome to the KKK Timer — a free web tool built to help you track and improve your solve times. You will be provided with many advanced tools for free without signing in. You can share sessions too. Please rate this timer if you like. I will fell glad to say that this timer helped you

This timer highly focuses on user's comfort plus user's improvement. Please make this timer spread across the world if you want to help other speedcubers. There are basic instructions given below

Instructions

Timer

Mobile: hold screen 0.5s until green, release to start, tap to stop.

Desktop: hold SPACEBAR 0.5s until green, release to start, any key to stop.

Music

50 unique tracks. Pick one or hit "Turn Off" for silence. you can choose your tracks. Please off the music if you want silent practice.

Sessions

Create multiple sessions to separate cubes or practice styles.

Inspection

Enable 15s inspection in Settings → Features. During inspection you can start the solve early by holding Spacebar — no penalty. If time runs out, +2 applies and timer starts automatically.

Penalties

Click any solve in the session list to apply +2 or DNF.
