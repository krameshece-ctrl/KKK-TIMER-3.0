How to Use KKK Timer 3.0

Complete guide — controls, stats, puzzles, and features.

Starting a solve

📱 Mobile / touchscreen

  1. Touch and hold anywhere on the timer area for about half a second.
  2. The timer turns red, then green — that means "ready."
  3. Release to start the timer.
  4. When you finish the solve, tap once anywhere to stop.

💻 Desktop / keyboard

  1. Hold the Spacebar for about half a second until it turns green.
  2. Release the spacebar to start the timer.
  3. Press any key to stop the timer when you finish.

15-second inspection

Enable inspection in Settings → Features → 15s Inspection Time. When enabled:

  • The timer counts down from 15 before each solve.
  • You can start solving early at any time — hold and release as normal, no penalty.
  • If the countdown reaches zero, a +2 penalty is applied and the timer starts automatically.

Stats explained

Best
Your fastest single solve in the current session (DNFs excluded).
Mo3 (Mean of 3)
Straight average of your last 3 solves. Any DNF makes the whole Mo3 a DNF.
Ao5, Ao12, Ao50, Ao100 (Average of N)
Trimmed averages. The fastest and slowest solves are dropped, and the rest are averaged. Two or more DNFs in the window make the average a DNF. These are the standard WCA-style averages.
Count
Number of solves recorded in the current session.

Tap any average card (Ao3, Ao5, etc.) to see the individual solves that make up that average.

Penalties (+2 and DNF)

Click any solve in the session list to open the penalty menu:

  • OK — removes any existing penalty.
  • +2 — adds 2 seconds to the solve.
  • DNF — marks the solve as Did Not Finish (excluded from best/avg calculations, but counted toward Ao/DNF rules).

Supported puzzles

Select your puzzle from the bar at the bottom of the timer. Scramble length adjusts automatically:

  • 2×2 — 11 moves (WCA style)
  • 3×3 — 20 moves
  • 4×4 — 40 moves
  • 5×5 — 60 moves
  • 6×6 — 80 moves
  • 7×7 — 100 moves
  • Skewb — 10 moves (Skewb notation)

Sessions

Sessions let you keep separate statistics for different puzzles or practice styles. Use the dropdown in the top bar to switch sessions, the + button to create a new one, and the trash icon to delete the current one.

Every session stores its own solves, stats, and averages. Switching puzzles (2×2 → 3×3, etc.) switches to that puzzle's session automatically.

Built-in cube assistant

Click the chat icon (top left) to open the assistant. It can help with:

  • Scramble analysis and opening-move advice
  • Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL tips
  • Time-target coaching (sub-30, sub-20, sub-15…)
  • Stats interpretation (Ao5, Ao12 gaps, consistency)
  • Finger tricks, look-ahead, TPS

Music and themes

50 focus music tracks are available in the bottom bar — pick one or tap Turn Off for silence. 25 color themes are available in Settings → 🎨 Theme. Your choice is saved automatically.

Sharing a session

Click the share icon (top bar) to send your current session's solves and scrambles to WhatsApp, Telegram, email, or copy them to your clipboard.

Privacy

All solves, sessions, and settings are stored locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. See the privacy page for details.
