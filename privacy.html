Privacy Policy

KKK Timer 3.0 is designed to collect nothing. This page explains exactly what is stored and where.

What is stored

All of the following is stored only in your browser using localStorage:

  • Your solves (times, scrambles, penalties, dates)
  • Your sessions and their names
  • Your chosen theme and inspection setting

What is NOT collected

  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No cookies
  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No advertising
  • No solve data sent to any server
  • No IP logging beyond what GitHub Pages itself does for hosting

Third-party services

The timer loads two external resources:

  • Tailwind CSS (CDN) — for styling
  • Google Fonts — for the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces

These services may log standard web requests (IP, user agent) per their own privacy policies. No solve data is ever sent to them.

Clearing your data

You can clear all stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Contact
